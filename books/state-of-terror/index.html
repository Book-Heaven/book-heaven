The book

About the book

After a new president appoints a political rival, Ellen Adams, as secretary of state, a series of apparent terrorist attacks begins. They turn out to be part of a larger international plot involving Pakistan, Afghanistan, Iran, nuclear weapons development, the Russian mob and a rogue terrorist group. Adams, a young Foreign Service officer and a journalist work to uncover and stop the conspiracy.

The author

About the author

Hillary Rodham Clinton was the 67th U.S. secretary of state. Louise Penny is a multiple award-winning, number one New York Times bestselling novelist.
