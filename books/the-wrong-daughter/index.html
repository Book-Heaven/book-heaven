The book

About the book

Sixteen years after ten-year-old Olivia disappears from her bedroom, her family is stunned when a woman claiming to be Olivia returns. Her younger sister Caitlin remembers the night differently and is unsure whether the woman is really her sister. As both women keep secrets and memories fail to match, the family must face the possibility that welcoming the wrong daughter home could destroy them all.

The author

About the author

Dandy Smith is a bestselling psychological-thriller author. She studied Creative Writing at Bath Spa University, completing both undergraduate and master's degrees. Her novels have been published internationally and translated into several languages; she lives in Somerset, England.
