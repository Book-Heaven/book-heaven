The book

About the book

After losing her job and apartment, Julia Barlow moves into Pemberton, her family's 17th-century estate in rural Massachusetts, to care for her grandmother. The house turns hostile: a clock stops at the same hour each night, lights flicker and a voice calls for help. As she digs into the estate's past, she learns of a woman executed for witchcraft in 1692 and uncovers family secrets.

The author

About the author

Freida McFadden is a physician based in Boston and the author of The Housemaid. Her publisher says her novels have sold over 90 million copies and been translated into more than 45 languages.
