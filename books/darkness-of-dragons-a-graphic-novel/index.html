About the book

Qibli knows the ancient dragon Darkstalker must be stopped. He believes he might be able to change the future with the power of Darkstalker's scroll, but the prophecies are difficult to rewrite. Using the small animus objects he has borrowed from Turtle, Qibli must decide whether he can become the magical force Pyrrhia needs—or risk bringing disaster to Jade Mountain, his friends and his family. This is the tenth Wings of Fire graphic-novel adaptation, illustrated by Mike Holmes.

The author

About the author

Tui T. Sutherland is the author of the Wings of Fire series. Mike Holmes illustrates this graphic-novel adaptation, bringing the dragon characters and Pyrrhia's world to the page.
