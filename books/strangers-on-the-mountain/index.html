The book

About the book

When a woman is attacked outside a bar, FBI agent Aurora Gray is called to investigate. The main suspect disappears into the mountains, where his burned-out camper and body are later found. As more missing men and victims emerge, Aurora discovers that each man was hiding a troubled past—and that someone may be offering desperate people a way to disappear and start over. The apparent escape could be a deadly trap, and Aurora must find the person hunting them before another victim vanishes.

The author

About the author

A. J. Rivers writes mystery and thriller fiction, often combining suspense, crime investigations and unexpected twists. Her author biography describes a lifelong interest in mysteries and crime-solving stories, with inspiration drawn from researching true crime.
