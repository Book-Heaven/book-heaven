The book

About the book

Growing Up Pentecostal: A Memoir explores Phil Matarese's childhood in a Pentecostal environment, the spiritual questions that followed him into adulthood, and the way his understanding of faith developed over time. Phil recalls spontaneous church services, later experiences in more structured Christian settings, and the moment visits from high-school friends made him see his familiar church through a different perspective.

The author

About the author

Phil Matarese is the author of Growing Up Pentecostal: A Memoir. In writing it, he waited until he felt able to approach his experiences with honesty, balance and respect. He made careful choices to protect the privacy of others, including changing names and leaving out stories that were too personal to share.

Why the book matters
