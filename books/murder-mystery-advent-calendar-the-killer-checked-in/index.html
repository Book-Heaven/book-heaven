About the book

An interactive Christmas murder-mystery challenge for adults built around the Kingsmoor hotel. The premise invites readers to work through 24 clues, consider 9,600 suspects and identify the one killer. It is presented as an advent-calendar-style mystery experience rather than a conventional novel.
