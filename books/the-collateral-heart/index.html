About the book

A devastating arson in Manhattan leaves a building destroyed and a trail of clues that may reveal where the arsonist will strike next. Forensic criminalist Lincoln Rhyme and detective Amelia Sachs investigate, turning to a popular history podcaster for help. Their case intersects with Long Island detective Desdemona Vale, who is searching for the killer of a young woman from her neighbourhood. As the investigations converge, the team must uncover the suspect's larger plan before more lives are lost.

The author

About the author

Jeffery Deaver is an internationally bestselling thriller writer and the creator of the Lincoln Rhyme series. He has written more than forty novels, and his work has been translated into numerous languages and published around the world.
