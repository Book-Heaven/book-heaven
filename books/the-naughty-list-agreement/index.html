The book

About the book

Cora Quill, the Queen of Christmas, needs a new toy maker after her supplier is caught up in a scandal. The only person who can help is Nick Bari—the grumpy toymaker she fired years earlier. Nick is also the current Santa Claus, and he must find a wife before Christmas Eve to keep the holiday season alive. Their festive deal brings two stubborn people together, with Christmas magic, rivalry and romance along the way.

The author

About the author

Katherine Garbera is a USA Today bestselling author of more than 145 novels. Her romances are known for big emotions, humour, twists and happily-ever-after endings. She lives in Kent, England.
