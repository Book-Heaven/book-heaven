FOR AUTHORS

Author interview opportunities

Share your writing journey in your own words. We welcome independent, emerging and traditionally published authors.

Who can apply?

Any author with an upcoming or published book can introduce it to Book Heaven. Tell us about the book and the conversation you would like to have.

Is there a fee?

Submitting a book for editorial consideration is free, and there is no fee to be considered for an editorial interview. Payment does not guarantee selection or publication.

How the interview is conducted

  1. Send your details: your name, contact email, book title, a link and a short note.
  2. We review them: we consider the book’s subject, genre and fit with Book Heaven.
  3. We send questions: if selected, you receive a written Q&A by email. A live conversation can be arranged separately if both sides prefer.
  4. You answer in your own words: we share the edited draft with you.
  5. You approve it: we confirm the answers and facts with you before publication.

What we may ask about

  • The idea or experience that started the book.
  • Research, difficult decisions and surprises along the way.
  • The characters and themes that matter most to you.
  • What you hope readers carry with them.
  • What you are working on next.

What we do not promise

We do not promise sales, reviews, audience numbers or a particular level of exposure.
