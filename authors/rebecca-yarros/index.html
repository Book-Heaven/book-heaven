About the author

Rebecca Yarros is a bestselling author of more than twenty novels, including Fourth Wing and In the Likely Event. Her work has earned the British Book Award for Book of the Year and the American Library Association's Alex Award. She lives in Colorado and co-founded the nonprofit One October in 2019, which supports children in the foster system.
