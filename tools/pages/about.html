OUR STORY & EDITORIAL APPROACH

Every book has a story beyond its pages.

Book Heaven helps readers discover books—and gives the people who create them a thoughtful place to share the stories behind their work.

Who is behind Book Heaven?

Book Heaven is an independent book-discovery and author-feature publication, created and managed by its founder as a reader-first editorial project. It is built around a simple belief: books deserve to be discovered for the ideas, imagination and human experience they carry. We aim to make the site useful to readers while treating authors and their work with care.

What we do

We curate book discoveries, publish book spotlights, introduce authors and develop thoughtful conversations about writing, creativity and publishing. Our library helps readers explore titles across genres—not rank writers or declare one book better than another.

How our editorial process works

  1. Discovery: We find books through reader interest, public book listings, publisher and author information, and direct submissions.
  2. Review: We check the information supplied, consider the book’s fit for the site and decide whether we can prepare a useful, accurate feature.
  3. Author contact: When a feature or interview needs the author’s participation, we contact them and explain what we are proposing.
  4. Preparation: We write the book spotlight or send interview questions. Authors can clarify factual details and approve their own interview answers before publication.
  5. Publication: Selected features are published on Book Heaven and may be shared through our reader updates.

Our editorial promise

We do not invent author endorsements, interviews, reviews or quotations. A sample is labelled as a sample. We aim to distinguish editorial discovery from advertising, verify factual details where possible and make no promises about book sales, reviews or audience numbers.

For readers and authors

Readers can browse the library, explore author pages and join the Reading Circle. Authors and publishers can use the submission page to introduce a title or express interest in a conversation. Every submission is considered on its merits; sending us a book does not guarantee publication.
