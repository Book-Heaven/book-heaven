ABOUT BOOK HEAVEN

Book recommendations, features and author stories.

Book Heaven is an independent book-discovery and author-feature publication, created and managed by its founder. We curate book recommendations, publish book features and author spotlights, and make room for writers to talk about their writing journeys.

For readers: browse the book library, meet the featured authors, or read the latest editorial feature.

For authors: we welcome independently published and traditionally published writers. Submitting a book for editorial consideration is free, and there is no fee to be considered for an editorial interview. Our standard interview is a written Q&A by email. Selection and publication are not guaranteed.

A LITTLE CLARITY

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of books do you feature?

Book Heaven features a varied selection of fiction and non-fiction, including romance, mystery, thrillers, fantasy, literary fiction, memoirs and other interesting reads. Our aim is to help readers discover engaging books and authors across different genres.

How do you choose which books to feature?

We consider the book’s subject, genre, premise, author and the details available about the title. We aim to present a balanced mix of familiar genres and fresh discoveries. A feature is an opportunity for readers to explore a book; it is not a promise of sales or a paid review.

Can an author request to be featured?

Yes. Authors can contact Book Heaven through the Submit Your Book section and share their book details for consideration. Please include accurate information about the title and author. Submitting a book does not guarantee publication or a feature; we review submissions before making a decision.

Are author submissions or interviews free?

Submitting a book for editorial consideration is free. Being considered does not guarantee a feature or interview. Book Heaven does not promise sales, reviews or a particular level of exposure.

How do Book Heaven author interviews work?

Our standard format is a written Q&A by email. We send the questions, the author responds in their own words, and we confirm the final answers and factual details with them before publication. We publish author answers in the writer’s own voice and confirm interview details before publication.

Where can I buy the books I find here?

Open the book’s “Get it” or retailer link on its page to visit the seller’s website. There you can check available formats, current pricing and purchasing options. Purchases take place on the retailer’s website, and prices or availability may change.

How can I stay up to date?

Use the Reading Circle email form above this FAQ section to subscribe. You’ll receive the weekly reading shortlist and updates about new book features and author conversations. Make sure you enter a valid email address.
