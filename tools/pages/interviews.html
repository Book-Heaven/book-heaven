BOOK HEAVEN CONVERSATIONS

Every author has more to tell than the story on the shelf.

At Book Heaven, our conversations go beyond a book description. We make space for authors to speak about the moment an idea began, the characters who stayed with them, the challenges behind the writing and the meaning they hope readers will discover.

These are genuine author conversations shaped around the writer’s own voice and experience. We want readers to finish an interview feeling closer to the person behind the pages—and curious to discover the book for themselves.

One of the stories in our author-conversation journey

Carly Robyn · Icy Control

Book Heaven has connected its author-conversation work with Carly Robyn and her book Icy Control. We are building this space to give each author feature the attention it deserves, with the book, the writer and the story at the centre.

What we ask authors about

  • The beginning: The spark, experience or question that inspired the book.
  • The creative journey: The research, difficult decisions and unexpected turns behind the writing.
  • The people and ideas: The characters, themes and emotions that matter most to the author.
  • The reader connection: What the author hopes readers will think about, feel or carry with them.
  • What comes next: New projects, lessons learned and advice for other writers.

Thoughtful questions. The author’s own voice.

Our conversations are prepared with care and respect for each writer. We work with authors to confirm details and present their words clearly. We do not manufacture quotations or put words in an author’s mouth.
