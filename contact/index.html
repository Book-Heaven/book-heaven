FOR AUTHORS & PUBLISHERS

Your book deserves a thoughtful introduction.

Tell us about your book, suggest an author conversation or ask a question. We welcome submissions from independently published and traditionally published authors.

What happens after you submit?

  1. You send the details. Share your name, contact email, book title, a link and a short note about the book.
  2. We review the submission. We consider the genre, subject, available information and fit with Book Heaven. We may contact you if we need clarification.
  3. We decide on a suitable feature. This could be a book listing, a written spotlight or an invitation to take part in a conversation. Not every submission will be selected.
  4. We agree the details with you. For an interview, we send questions by email. You write your answers in your own words, and we share the edited draft for your approval before it goes live. A live conversation can be arranged separately if both sides prefer.
  5. Your feature is published. If selected, we publish it on Book Heaven and may include it in our reader updates.

Is it free?

Submitting a book for editorial consideration is free. There is no fee to be considered for an editorial interview, and payment does not guarantee selection or publication. We do not promise sales, reviews or a particular level of exposure.

How are interviews conducted?

Our standard format is a written Q&A by email, so authors can answer thoughtfully and at a convenient time. We send the questions, the author responds in their own voice, then we confirm the final answers and factual details with them before publication. We never invent answers or publish a fictional interview as a real one.

Submitting this form is a request for consideration, not a guarantee of publication. We will use your details to respond to your submission.
